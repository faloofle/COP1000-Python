Investment Portfolio Analysis

-Description:
  -The data that is analized is the total lifespan of an investments, including
  any deviation from the original investment such as momentarily gaining/losing
  value. The total amount of loss/decline and gain/increase the investment goes
  through for the entire investment lifespan.
  -Selected the data so that I could evaluate an investment portfolio. The net 
  losses and gains are more important than the total amount of loss and gain, 
  however the totals can be used to identify market volitality, whereas the nets
  are just used to find the completed investment value
  -My program analyzes the initial investment along with the total amount of 
  loss and gain the investment experienced over the life of the investment. It
  uses those values and calculates the total profits, total losses, net gains,
  net losses, roi, average investment, and highest profit/highest loss   
-Dataset
  -Investment is the original investment amount that someone has put in.
  The gain is the total amount of money that investment gained over the life
  of such investment, not net gain. The loss is the total amount of money that
  investment lost over the life of such investment, not net loss. With these
  values we can calculate the net profit/loss.   
-Analysis
 -My code calculates net profit/loss, total investments, total gains/losses,
 average investment cost, the highest gain/loss and the roi   
-Visualization
 -I used a bar graph to depict the net profit/loss of each investment, It allows
 me to compare each investment net to eachother, and allows it to be understood
 easily. The x axis is the number of investments, and the y axis is the net
 profit/loss of each investment
